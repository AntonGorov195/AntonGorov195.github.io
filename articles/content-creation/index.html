Be a Active, Not Just Passive

This is an article for those who spend their free time consuming content rather than creating it. If you find yourself spend hours watching TokTok or reading blogs without any thoughtful engagement with the content, this article is for you. I personally find myself spending long stretches of time on the screen so I write this for myself too.

Passive vs. Active

There is a spectrum of how we engage with the things we experience. On the one side we are passively watching them. If you watch a movie or keep reading this article, you are most likely a passive consumer. On the other side we are actively engaging with content. Me writing this article is me being active and if you write a review for this article you would be active as well. It is a spectrum because not all content requires the same level of engagement. Games for example, may have a wide range of how much a play is expected to engage with. E.g. Incremental game are more passive than action games. Any activity has a passive and active element to it.

Do not be mistaken, there is nothing wrong with passive content. It is fine to just sit and enjoy a movie without taking dozen of notes and analyzing every detail. It is important to take time and relax.

Comfort is not the problem but the lack of discomfort. Today it is easier than ever to drown in passive content consumption. There is so much of it and very little punishment for over indulging in it. But what is wrong with passivity?

Downsides of Not Thinking

Let's start with how it affects us on a personal level. It erodes your thinking skill. I personally feel dumber after going on TikTok even for a short time. I'm confident that many people feel the same. And the ones who don't were brain washed to accept their stupidity.

I would go even further than that. Our capacity for independant thought is an integral part of the human experience. By succumbing to lack of thinking, we become less human. You are an inherently worse human being if you can't resist passive consumption.

On a societal level over passivity destroys our culture. The current era of slop can only exist in a passive consumption society. If people were to actively engage with content, they would realize there is nothing to engage with. Try writing an in depth review of an AI generated TikTok short. There is not much there to review. All "style" no substance.

This means that people who do care about thinking have less avenues to find thoughtful material. Slop hurts all of us, directly or indirectly. This is why I don't use AI in the making of this article. I'm writing it primarily for myself.

What Can We Do? Accept Skill Issues

Start making things yourself. Stop judging them for their quality and start judging yourself by the effort you put in. This article might not be the pinnacle of humanity, but it is better than doing nothing.

Be active with how you view content. Write in depth reviews on what you watch and if you can't, stop watching it. Put effort into things even if your are bad at them. Go create, not for others but for yourself.
