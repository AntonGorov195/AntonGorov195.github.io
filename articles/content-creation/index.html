Be a Creator, Not a Just Consumer

This is an article for those who spend their free time consuming content rather than creating it. If you find yourself spend hours watching TokTok or reading blogs without any thoughtful engagement with the content, this article is for you. I personally find myself spending long stretches of time on the screen so I write this for myself too.

My main point is that many of us spend too much time being passive rather than active. By being an active participant you get way more meaningful experiences. Of course this isn't easy to be active, but an easy life is also a bad one.

Creator vs. Consumer

First we need to establish clear definitions for important words in this article.

  1. Content - Something a human can experience (food, movies, web articles, etc.).
  2. Creator - Someone who creates content.
  3. Consumer - Someone who experiences content.

Nowdays most people (myself included) spend their time consuming content rather than creating it (citation needed). This isn't a bad thing, quite the opposite. Life is about having meaningful experiences. Your high school graduation, your birthday parties, time spend with your family and so much more are experiences There is no difference between content and an experience as outlined by the definition above. that make life great.

However, not all content is the same. Most TikTok videos have nearly no value (or even negative value Most of the time I look at X(twitter) or TikTok feel dumber then before.) beyond being addicting. It is important to identify low meaning content and avoid it.
